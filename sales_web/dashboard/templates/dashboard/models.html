Pipeline Architecture
Chronological Split

Data is partitioned into 80% historical training (Jan 2023–May 2025) and 20% out-of-sample testing (Jun 2025–Dec 2025) strictly preventing future data leakage in time-series forecasting.

Feature Engineering

Engineered features include temporal flags (day_of_week, month, is_weekend), cyclical seasonality encoding, lag features (7 & 14 days), and 7-day rolling momentum.

Why Random Forest Won

Non-linear decision boundaries and ensemble variance reduction enabled Random Forest to model holiday spikes and weekend demand without overfitting to local noise.
